# Privacy Policy — Qibla

_Last updated: September 24, 2026_

Qibla is designed to work entirely on your device.

**Location.** If you allow it, Qibla asks your device for your approximate location. The
app uses it to calculate the Qibla direction, the distance to Makkah and prayer times. You
can instead choose a city yourself. Your location is stored only in the app's local
storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

**Room alignment.** When you set north, the app may save a spatial anchor through your
headset's system. That anchor is managed by the device and stays on it. Qibla does not
upload it.

**No other data.** Qibla has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. It makes no
network requests beyond loading the app itself.

**Deleting your data.** Uninstall the app, or clear its site data, to remove everything it
has stored.
